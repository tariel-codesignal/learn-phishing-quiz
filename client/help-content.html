Overview

Welcome to the CodeSignal Phishing Quiz. You will walk through realistic messages that mirror the email inbox, Slack, and SMS apps you already use. For each one, decide whether it is Phishing or Legit and learn what gave it away.

Your name and email are already filled in as learner / learner@codesignal.com. Keep them or personalize them so the scenarios address you directly—either way you can start immediately.

How the Quiz Works

  1. Landing card: Review the short intro message, confirm your name/email, and hit Take the Quiz. If something is missing, you will see a small error note under the field.
  2. Review the scenario: Each card explains the channel and sender at the top, followed by the simulated interface. Read carefully—hovering or tapping links reveals the real URL without opening it.
  3. Make your call: Use the red Phishing or blue Legit button. They stay clickable even if you answer quickly, so feel free to change your mind before moving on.
  4. Check the explanation: After you choose, you will see whether you were correct, two highlight bullets, and a short explanation. Use Review Scenario to reopen the message or Next Scenario to continue.
  5. Finish strong: The final summary shows your score, accuracy, and every scenario status. Hit Restart quiz if you want to run through the set again right away.

The progress text in the header (“Question X of 6” or “Training complete”) tells you exactly where you are.

Email, Slack, & SMS

Email view

  • Modeled after Gmail with sender avatars, “to me” toggles, security headers, and a link preview strip at the bottom.
  • Scenario notes such as External, Reply-To, or doc attachments appear exactly where you would see them in a real inbox.
  • Look for mismatched domains, urgent tone, or attachment requests that do not fit the story.

Slack view

  • Shows a full Slack workspace with channel list, sender badges (APP, Verified, External), attachments, reactions, and thread counts.
  • Notice whether the bot/app is verified, if an external org badge appears, or if the domain on attachments looks suspicious.
  • Even though links are safe to click here, act as you would in Slack: hover to inspect the destination first.

SMS view

  • Uses an iMessage-style phone frame so you can focus on the sender label, number, and message bubble.
  • Short URLs, payment demands, and strange callback numbers are common warning signs.
  • Reply buttons are disabled—this is a safe sandbox to practice spotting suspicious texts.

Links & Formatting

The scenarios support a small set of formatting options so the messages feel real:

  • Markdown-style links such as [Security Center](https://example.com) appear as normal links with the true URL in the status bar.
  • **Bold** and _italic_ text highlight urgent phrases just like you would see in an email or Slack post.
  • SMS messages keep things simple: plain text with preserved line breaks.
  • Every link is blocked from navigating away from the quiz, so you can inspect any destination safely.

Tips for Success

  • Slow down: Scams often rely on panic. Take a breath and look for details that do not match the story.
  • Check the domain: Hover over links or look at the email headers to confirm who really sent the message.
  • Cross-channel clues: Finance or HR rarely ask for urgent action over random texts or Slack bots. Use another trusted channel to verify.
  • Use the recap: The red-flag list on the result screen explains every call so you can learn from misses immediately.
  • Need to start over? The “CodeSignal Phishing Quiz” logo in the header takes you back to the welcome card at any time.

FAQ

Do I have to change the name and email?

No. They are pre-filled with learner@codesignal.com so you can jump right in. Update them only if you want the messages to use different details.

What happens when I finish?

You will see a summary card with your score and each scenario’s status. You can restart immediately or close the quiz when you are done.

Where can I see the explanations again?

On the result screen, press Review Scenario to revisit the full message, or finish the quiz to review the final checklist. This Help modal is always available from the header.
