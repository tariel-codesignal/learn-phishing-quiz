Overview

Can You Spot the Phish? is a phishing awareness simulator that walks you through realistic email, SMS, and Slack messages. Your goal is to label each scenario as phishing or legitimate and then review the red flags that explain the correct answer.

Getting Started

  1. Select Begin Training on the welcome screen.
  2. Review the fake interface carefully. Hovering over links (on desktop) shows the URL in the preview bar.
  3. Choose Phishing or Legit. The result screen reveals the red flags.
  4. Use the Next Scenario button to continue until you finish all six cases.

Common Warning Signs

  • Sender mismatch: Look beyond the display name and inspect the full email or phone number.
  • Urgency & fear: Threats of account closure or penalties are classic manipulation tactics.
  • Unexpected attachments or links: Verify the actual domain before downloading or clicking.
  • Grammar issues: Awkward phrasing or odd capitalization can signal a scam.
  • Unusual channel: Finance mandates rarely arrive via SMS or random Slack bots.

Quiz Workflow

  1. Briefing: Read the welcome guidance and start when ready.
  2. Scenario review: Inspect sender details, timestamps, and interface cues.
  3. Decision: Submit your judgement with the action buttons.
  4. Debrief: Study the red flags list and explanation before moving on.
  5. Final report: After the sixth scenario, your score summary appears and is logged for the AI validator.

Pro Tips

  • Compare email domains with official company domains before trusting a link.
  • Slack bots should come from verified apps; when in doubt, confirm in another channel.
  • SMS alerts about money or credentials are extremely rare - verify via the official mobile app.
  • Use the sidebar progress list to revisit a scenario context before answering.
  • Hit ESC at any time to close this help modal.

FAQ

What happens if I mislabel a scenario?

The explanation will highlight the clues you missed so you can improve next time.

Are these scenarios based on real incidents?

They are inspired by real phishing attempts but scrubbed of sensitive data.

Does the training store my answers?

The current version only logs the structured results in the browser console for the AI evaluator stub.
